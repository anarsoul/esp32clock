onebitdawn: the licences of what a release is built from
=========================================================

onebitdawn itself is Copyright (c) 2026 Vasily Khoruzhick and licensed
under MIT or Apache-2.0, at your option:
https://github.com/anarsoul/onebitdawn

The files of a release also hold other people's work:

- The firmware images, the pack maker (onebitdawn-pack-*) and its
  WebAssembly on the pack maker's page are built from the Rust crates
  listed below, each under the licence it is listed with. The list is
  made for every system a release is built for, so not every crate is
  in every file.
- The whole-flash image (onebitdawn-<tag>.bin) and the web installer's
  bootloader.bin hold the ESP-IDF second-stage bootloader, Copyright
  Espressif Systems (Shanghai) CO LTD, Apache-2.0:
  https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf
- The firmware links Espressif's Wi-Fi, PHY and coexistence
  libraries, binaries that come in the esp-wifi-sys-esp32c3 crate
  below, Copyright Espressif Systems (Shanghai) CO LTD, Apache-2.0:
  https://github.com/espressif/esp32-wifi-lib
  https://github.com/espressif/esp-phy-lib
  https://github.com/espressif/esp-coex-lib
- Among those binaries is ESP-IDF's build of wpa_supplicant and
  hostapd, under the BSD licence that is given in full after this
  list:
  https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/tree/master/components/wpa_supplicant
- A crate published with no licence file of its own is listed under
  the plain text of its licence, with no copyright line; its authors
  are at the address beside its name.
- The clip packs (onebitdawn-clips-*.bin) are music and not under the
  project's licence; each clip's author and terms are in
  https://github.com/anarsoul/onebitdawn/blob/master/clips/README.md
